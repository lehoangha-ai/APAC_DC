Step 4 · Excel Pass/Fail Checks

5 Structural Verifications

Allowable limits driven by Step 1 country + Step 2 system selection

Active design basis (Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 4)
— a. Ultimate Limit State — Bending Stress σ = Mmax/Wx ≤ γc·fy = 270 MPa
— b. Serviceability — Frame Deflection y ≤ L/240 = H/240 mm
— c. Gypsum Board Stress σmax ≤ [σ] = 2.2 MPa
— d-1. Anchor Bolt — Shear Qmax ≤ [Q] = 3,700 N (M6, d=30mm)
— d-2. Anchor Bolt — Axial / Pullout Nmax ≤ [N]=2,850N & [S]=1,200N
TCVN 5575-2024 §7.2 AISC 360-16 Gypsum Construction Handbook Ch.2 HAS Expansion Anchor data
